Party Stash v1.2

One infinite stash shared by the whole party, Pillars of Eternity style, built into a redesigned inventory page. For Baldur's Gate: Enhanced Edition (with or without Siege of Dragonspear), Baldur's Gate II: Enhanced Edition and EET.

Features

  • Shared stash: every party member reaches the same infinite stash on the inventory page. Quests still see the items stored in it.
  • New inventory page: backpack and stash in one scrollable grid, a ground column, compact 2×2 stats (attributes in 3 letters).
  • Tabs: All, Weapons, Armor, Gear, Ammo, Gems, Scrolls, Potions, Quest, Misc, Bags.
  • Sorting: Auto (each tab its own order: weapons by damage per round for the shown character, armor by AC, scrolls by class and level…), Date, Quality, Type, Price, Value/weight. Right-click the sort button to reverse it.
  • Hints: red = unusable by the shown character, blue = unidentified, + = an upgrade for him (or a scroll a party mage can still learn).
  • Marks: Ctrl + click an item (stash, backpack or equipped) to mark it for sale (darkened), again to make it valuable (golden), again to clear it. Sell Marked sells all the items marked for sale at once at any merchant, with the total on the button.
  • Damage per round: weapon tooltips show the damage per round and THAC0 for the shown character (strength, proficiencies, attacks per round, both hands when dual-wielding); the stats show the attacks per round.
  • Charges: wands and other charged items show their charges in the corner of their cell.
  • Valuable items: never sold, skipped by Store all and by the merchants' selection, and they cannot be dropped on the ground. A backpack item marked for sale goes straight to the stash.
  • Put away: right-click the big bag next to the weight, then open a container, and choose to put the backpack or the shown stash page in it (valuable and quest items stay).
  • Options (separate components): the stash weighs nothing; keep the party gold through the campaign changes (BG1 to SoD, SoD to BG2 in EET); hardcore: those changes empty the stash too.
  • Bags: a bag put in the stash is emptied into it and kept in the Bags tab; the stash items it can hold weigh nothing (a Bag of Holding: the whole stash).
  • Weight: the stash counts against the sum of the members' weight allowances; above it, the party is slowed down.
  • Two looks: Baldur's Gate 1 or Baldur's Gate II style, on either game.
  • Sorted loot (optional): ground piles, containers and the loot bar list quest items, then weightless items, then the rest by value/weight.

Requirements

Game version v2.6.6 or v2.7.x, and EEex (installed first). A mod that replaces the inventory screen (Dragonspear UI++…) conflicts with the new page. LeUI works with Party Stash: pick the Baldur's Gate II look. Infinity UI++ installs and runs in our tests; feedback welcome.

Installation

  1. Install EEex.
  2. Extract the archive into the game folder (where chitin.key is).
  3. Run setup-PartyStash.exe, pick your language, choose a look (the one matching your game is recommended) and, if you like, Sorted loot.
  4. Start the game with InfinityLoader.exe (EEex).

Uninstalling

Empty the stash first, in game: Ctrl + left click on the big bag next to the weight. Your collected bags come back filled with the items they can hold; everything else drops on the ground. Then run setup-PartyStash.exe and uninstall. Otherwise the stash contents stay in your saves but cannot be reached until the mod is reinstalled.

FAQ

How does sorting work? Each tab has its own default sort (Auto: weapons by damage per round, armor by AC, scrolls by class and level…), or you pick Date, Quality, Type, Price or Gp/lb.

What about bags, even bags of holding? There is no nesting. A bag put in the stash is emptied into it and kept in the Bags tab, so the stash stays one flat list. Each collected bag makes the stash items it could hold weightless. A bag of holding covers every type: with one, the whole stash weighs nothing.

I pick up every single thing. Is that a problem? The stash is infinite. Without a bag of holding, its weight counts against the party's combined allowance (above it, everyone slows down). Store all empties a backpack in one click, and Sell Marked sells everything you marked at any merchant in one go.

Does it slow the game down? No per-creature hooks (no opcode, script or action hooks): its work is spaced out (a quick check every 500 ms, the party weight every 2 s), so the number of creatures on screen does not change its cost.

Notes

  • From Baldur's Gate to Siege of Dragonspear, the stash is carried over as long as the game is not closed between the end of BG1 and the start of SoD.
  • Translations are welcome: PartyStash/tra/.
  • EET (Enhanced Edition Trilogy): tested on v2.6.6. The stash survives the move from Siege of Dragonspear to Baldur's Gate II.
